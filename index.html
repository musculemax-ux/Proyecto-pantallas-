🧠 Mental Hacking: Dopamine & FOMO

Your brain is running the show behind the scenes. Meet the two culprits.

🎰

Scroll Addiction

Every like and notification releases dopamine, your brain's instant-pleasure hormone. That's why you open TikTok or Instagram every 5 minutes without even realizing it.

😰

FOMO: Fear of Missing Out

The constant fear of missing out, or being left out of the group, builds anxiety and stress every time you can't check your phone.

🧟 Physical Damage: Fatigue & Text Neck

Screens don't stay on the screen. They follow you into your sleep, your eyes and your spine.

🧟

Sleepless Zombies

–2 hrs

Blue light tricks your brain into thinking it's daytime, which stops melatonin (your sleep hormone) production. The damage: up to 2 hours of real rest stolen.

👁️

Dry Eyes

50% fewer blinks

You blink 50% less when you stare at a screen. Result? Epic headaches, blurry vision and red eyes.

🦴

Text Neck

27 kg

Tilting your head down to your phone puts up to 27 kilos (60 lbs) of weight on your neck. Your spine suffers and your posture gets destroyed.

📊 The Unseen Reality: Stats & Medical Facts

The numbers nobody puts in your feed.

⏱️

How Much Is Too Much?

3 hrs

The WHO recommends limiting recreational screen time. More than 3 hours a day just for fun is excessive.

🌐

Connected Life

6h 40m average per day

That's almost 1/3 of your day online, adding up to 100+ days a year on the internet. And 70% of teen screen time goes straight to social media and video.

📲

Checking Habits

96–150

The average person checks their phone 96 to 150 times a day. That's about every 10 minutes.

😱

Nomophobia

A very real (and totally irrational) medical fear of leaving home without your phone, battery or signal. Think rapid heartbeat and sweating.

👻

Phantom Vibration Syndrome

80% of users

Feel fake vibrations in their pocket. Your brain is on such high alert for notifications that it invents them.

🧽

The Google Effect (Digital Amnesia)

Why remember anything when it's one click away? Your brain stops trying, and your long-term memory gets weaker.

🤹

Fake Multitasking

–40%

Your brain can't do two things at once. Switching from homework to WhatsApp drops your productivity by 40%.

🫥

Social Isolation

Plot twist: more social media means more perceived loneliness, less empathy and a weaker ability to read body language.
